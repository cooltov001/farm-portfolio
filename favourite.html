MY FAVOURITE THINGS

A little collection of the things that make me happy

Favourite Food

My favourite food is Fried Rice.

I like it because it's carbs, protein, and vegetables all in one pan. One plate can feel like a full meal.

Favourite Color

My favourite colour is Red.

It makes me feel passionate and exciting.

Favourite Sport

My favourite sport is Football.

I enjoy it because one moment of brilliance or a single mistake can change everything. The game stays tense until the final whistle. Watching a well-drilled team move as one unit is deeply satisfying.

Favourite Musician

My favourite musician is Wizkid.

I love his music because his Afrobeats production is groovy without being forced. The drums, bass, and percussion create a feel that's easy to move to and hard to forget.

Favourite Movie

My favourite movie is John Wick.

I like it because the gun-fu and hand-to-hand fights are tightly choreographed, clear, and creative. You can actually follow what's happening, and it still looks brutal and stylish.
