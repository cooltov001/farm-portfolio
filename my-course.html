Full-Stack Web Development Course

This course trains students to build complete web applications from start to finish.
You will learn both frontend and backend development, covering how to create user interfaces, manage servers, work with databases, and connect everything into a functional product.

What you will learn:
Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and modern frameworks
Backend: Server-side programming, APIs, and databases
Full application development, deployment, and best practices
Practical projects that prepare you for real-world work

Class Schedule:

Monday – Thursday: 9:00 AM – 11:00 AM (Learning & Practical Sessions)
Friday: 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM (Project Presentations & Reviews)
The course focuses on hands-on learning. During the week you build skills and work on projects,
while Fridays are dedicated to presenting your work, receiving feedback, and improving through practical demonstration.
