How the Biogas System Works

1

Collect Waste

Pig and goat manure is collected daily from the pens.

2

Feed the Digester

Manure is mixed with water and fed into the sealed digester.

3

Gas Production

Bacteria break down the waste and produce methane-rich biogas.

4

Use the Benefits

Gas for cooking/electricity + bio-slurry for fertilizing crops.
