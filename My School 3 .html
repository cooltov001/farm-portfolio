OIC HUB

OIC Hub Osogbo is an IT consulting company and coding training institute based in Osogbo, Osun State, Nigeria.

It offers practical, hands-on training in web development (frontend, backend, and full-stack), data science, data analysis, AI/machine learning, UI/UX design, mobile and desktop app development, and basic computer skills.

The hub also provides software development services for businesses and runs coding programs for children and students.

Location: 2, Adekunle Square, beside Ebenezer Hotel, Fagbewesa Street, Olaiya, Osogbo. It focuses on equipping people with in-demand digital skills through physical, project-based learning.
